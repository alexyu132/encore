Use 4 M3x10mm bolts and 4 lock nuts to attach a Z top clamp piece to each side of the Z top bracket. These should be installed loosely for now.
![alt text](image-49.png)

Use 2 M3x10mm bolts and 2 lock nuts to attach the PCB endstop to the center of the bracket as shown. (Note: it's easier to have the cable already connected when attaching, as there's very little space next to the connector after it's mounted.)

![alt text](image-50.png)

Use 3x lock nuts and 3 M3x10mm bolts to attach the bracket to the rear midframe on the gantry.

## 3. Bed assembly

### Printed parts:
|Item|Qty|
|--|--|
|Bed support bracket|1|
| Z adjuster clamp | 1 | 

### Non-printed parts:
|Item|Qty|
|--|--|
| Voron 0 bed assembly| 1 |
| Leveling kit | 1 |
| LM8LUU bearing | 2 |
| T8 leadscrew nut | 1 |
| M3x10mm bolt | 9 |
| M3x25mm bolt |1 |
| M3 lock nut | 10 |

Use 4 M3x10mm bolts and lock nuts to attach the T8 leadscrew nut to the bed support bracket. Optionally, you can just use 2 bolts here. Leave them slightly loose to reduce the possibility of Z wobble.

![alt text](image-56.png)

Insert the LM8LUU bearings into the slots on the bed support bracket. The bottom of the bearings should be flush with the bottom of the bracket. Use 4 M3x10mm bolts plus lock nuts to clamp them in place. 

![alt text](image-57.png)


Insert an M3 lock nut into the slot in the Z adjuster clamp.

![alt text](image-58.png)

Insert an M3 lock nut into the slot in the bed support bracket as shown:
![alt text](image-59.png)

Attach the Z adjuster clamp to the bed support bracket using an M3x10mm bolt. 
![alt text](image-60.png)

Insert the M3x25mm bolt from above. This bolt will contact the Z endstop switch, and tightening/loosening it will adjust the Z offset. The captive lock nut inside the adjuster clamp keeps it fixed in place. Make sure the screw is unable to wobble - that'll make the Z offset inconsistent.

![alt text](image-62.png)

Using the leveling kit screws, attach the bed to the bed support bracket. The bed leveling springs (not shown) should be directly underneath the bed, and the bed leveling knobs should be underneath the bed support bracket.
![alt text](image-61.png)
